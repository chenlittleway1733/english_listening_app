## Lesson 4 題庫內容

依七上 Lesson 4 課本內容設計，包含：

- 單字與指令：listen, voice, stand up, sit down, take a photo, make noise
- 反義詞與常用語：quiet/noisy, early/late, hard/soft, young/old
- 對話內容：songs, live stream, sidewalk, cross the road, watch out, phubber
- 交通安全：traffic lights, ground, look down, all the time
- 文法：Can you...? / Yes, I can. / No, I can't.
- 能力問答：What can he/she/they do?
- 閱讀：Smart Designs, app, phone lanes, road safety
- Read Up：Rules for the Aquarium
- 發音重點：c/k/ck, g, f, v

## 使用方式

1. 選擇 `初級 Level 1 / Beginner`
2. 選擇 `七上 Grade 7-1 / 國中2000單字`
3. 選擇 `Lesson 4：I Can Listen to Their Songs Again and Again`
4. 選擇 `全部` 或 `錯題出題`
